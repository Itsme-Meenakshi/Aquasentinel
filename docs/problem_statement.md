# Problem statement

Pufferfish (*Lagocephalus* spp.) are reported to damage nets and catch along the Kerala coast (Mohamed et al., 2013).
Fishers and researchers lack a simple, evidence-based picture of **when and where pufferfish turn up in the catch**.

CMFRI supplied 253 ring-seine hauls (Sep 2023 to Jun 2026) near Kochi. Each haul records whether pufferfish were caught
(38 hauls) or confirmed absent (215 hauls).

**Question:** can the time of year, position, depth, gear and sea temperature tell us how likely a haul is to contain pufferfish?

**What this project is, honestly:** a pilot on a small dataset. It aims to (1) show what the data supports, (2) compare simple
models with honest baselines, tested on later hauls, and (3) wrap the result in a versioned, reviewable monitoring prototype
(the "twin") that refuses to answer where the data cannot support an answer.

**Out of scope:** net damage and economic loss (not in the data), forecasts for the whole Kerala coast (data covers one ground).
